Trust

Your books stay your books

An assistant that touches your accounts has to be careful about four things. These are the ones we were strict about.

Reads and navigation only, by default

The panel says so on every screen. Writing anything at all means deliberately switching to CoWork, and even then it only drafts.

We do not keep your data

AI Assist reads and writes through your own ERPNext API. Your invoices, parties and stock stay in your site. We store the connection, not the ledger.

It will not invent a total

When a list is longer than what it fetched, it says the list was cut short instead of reporting that number as the count.

Only mapped numbers get answers

A WhatsApp number has to be linked to an ERPNext user before it sees anything. An unknown number gets no data, whoever it belongs to.
